Flagship

Personalised fashion recommendations at scale

31.8M transactions · 1.37M customers · 105K products (H&M dataset)

Which twelve products should a retailer show each customer next week? Five retrieval methods shortlist about 145 candidates per customer, then a learning-to-rank model orders them using 53 customer, product and interaction features. Evaluated with rolling time-based validation and an untouched holdout week, against a bestseller baseline.

  • PySpark
  • LightGBM LambdaRank
  • Item-based CF
  • Graph embeddings
  • 214 tests
